Stuck?
The game. Each round you quote a bid and an ask (at most 2 apart). The trader across from you already knows the next piece of the answer. If your price is wrong they'll trade against you, and you'll lose. Sometimes a random customer trades too. Their trade is a clue: update your quote before the next reveal.
